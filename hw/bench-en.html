100 tasks, three roads, a supreme judge.

Every task was driven on three roads through Anthropic's real API: 🛵 scooter (Claude Haiku 5.5), 🚗 car (Claude Sonnet 5.5) and 🚛 truck (Claude Opus 5.5). Supreme judge (AI) = a third model (Claude Opus 5.5) that rules like a supreme court justice: it receives the answers unnamed and in random order, so it cannot know who wrote what and cannot favour the expensive one. It scores each answer 1–10 and explains its ruling. Every number on this page is computed live from the receipts at the bottom. The tasks themselves were written in Hebrew.

The finding: value moved from the chip to the route

Descartes said "I think, therefore I am" (Cogito Ergo Sum). SES says "I feel, therefore I am" (Sentio Ergo Sum). HybridWays turns the spotlight away from the chips, which save compute time, toward the information routes, which save time, money and calm for the person using them.

The algorithm: how each trip chooses a road

HybridWays is a hybrid of two agents: my data agent, which sits with the driver and keeps what it knows about them, and the road agent, the model on the chosen road, which does the task. Between them stands the Waze, which decides which road to take.

StepWhat happens
1. My data agentThe driver writes a task. Their agent keeps personal information on the driver's device, and the driver decides in advance what each road may know. A road without permission gets no personal detail at all.
2. The Waze (classifier)A small, cheap model (Haiku) reads the task and assigns one of 13 categories: shopping, translation, general info, travel, food, work, code, long writing, money, health, law, personal details, other.
3. Safety ruleMoney, health, law and personal details never go on the scooter, even when it is cheaper.
4. Drivers' mapAfter at least 3 reports in a category: if the scooter was enough 70% of the time or more, the Waze sends it to the scooter. Below 40%, it moves up to the car or the truck.
5. The driver's rulesA rule the driver set ("translation always by scooter") overrides the Waze. The driver can always pick a different road by hand.
6. Road agent🛵 Scooter, Claude Haiku 5.5: routine tasks, short wording, lists.
🚗 Car, Claude Sonnet 5.5: work, emails, planning, medium analysis.
🚛 Truck, Claude Opus 5.5: sensitive topics, deep analysis, long writing.
7. ReportThe driver taps 👍 or 👎. The report joins everyone's map, improves the Waze for the next driver and earns the driver a share of the savings pool.

In the open: the 100-task test did not use the personal data agent. All tasks were sent with no personal detail, so the comparison between roads stays clean. The drivers' map and rules run on the live proof page.
